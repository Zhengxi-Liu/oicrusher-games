    How it works

    • This is one school that never resets. Everyone who plays walks the same halls.
    • Aim at a desk, a shelf, the top of a locker or the floor and press H to hide a MacBook, a TI-84, a Hydro Flask, a Red Bull… with a note.
    • Your stash stays exactly there until someone finds it, even after you log off.
    • Within 2.5 m of a stash, press E to inspect it and read the note. Finders score 10 points plus 1 for every hour it stayed hidden; the hider scores too.

    🚫 Keyword filter

    English, pinyin and Chinese (Simplified / Traditional are matched the same). Changes apply immediately. Spaces, symbols, numbers, repeated letters, look-alike letters and full-width forms are normalised before matching.
    whole word only matches the keyword as a word of its own (so "class" never trips "ass"); inside words also matches within longer words. Chinese keywords always match inside text. "not when inside" lists phrases that make a match harmless.
    Words or phrases that are always fine, even though they contain a keyword.
    Everything the filter blocked (usernames, chat, notes, whiteboards), newest first, with the keywords that triggered it. Kept on the server in tjhs-modlog.jsonl.
