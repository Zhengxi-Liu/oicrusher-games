A level is complete when every terminal outputs its target for every combination of the switches you can flip. Click a row to set the switches to that combination.

Hint

Controls

  • Pick a gate, then click an empty cell to place it. Click a placed gate to rotate it 90°.
  • Drag from one pin to another to draw a wire, or click both pins. An output can drive many wires; an input takes only one.
  • Click a switch on the left to flip it between 0 and 1. Locked switches can’t be flipped.
  • Right-click (or use Erase) to delete gates and wires.
  • 1–4 pick gate · R rotate · E erase · Ctrl/⌘ Z undo · Esc cancel
