BOMBSIGHT

Click the map to choose the point you want to hit; wheel = zoom, drag = pan. The red cross is where a bomb released this instant would land (gravity, drag, your speed and the wind included); the blue ring is where along your track it would have to leave to land on the target. The bomb does not steer itself: scatter, gusts and anything you change before the doors open move the real impact. Enter = drop.

The aircraft is simulated, not animated: every surface makes lift and drag from its own angle of attack. Slow down too much or pull too hard and the wing stalls - release back pressure, add power and level the wings. Aerobatic planes stall at full back stick, so the keys pull only part way; hold Shift for full deflection. Approach at about 70 kt with flaps, follow the PAPI lights (two white, two red) and flare just above the runway.
